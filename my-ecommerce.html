ECOMMERCE PLATFORM

my-ecommerce

A complete ecommerce platform designed to manage the customer-facing store and the business operations behind it in one organized system.

PROJECT TYPE Personal Project
ROLE Full-Stack Developer
STATUS Version 1.1
FOCUS Ecommerce & Business Operations
THE CHALLENGE

More than just an online storefront.

The goal was to build a reusable ecommerce platform that could support real business operations, not only display products. The system needed to organize product information, inventory, customers, pricing and store management while remaining flexible enough to be reused for future ecommerce projects.

THE SOLUTION

One system for the store and its operations.

I designed the application structure and database, then developed both the administration side and customer-facing storefront. The project covers the core areas needed to manage an ecommerce business while keeping the application organized and extendable.
